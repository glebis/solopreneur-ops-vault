**Aleksandra Osipova** — AI practitioner / builder

**Why relevant:** You teach both of these skills structurally inside a cohort curriculum — not as mindset tips but as repeatable practices with specific exercises. The comment thread has appreciation but no depth; people are asking "how do I practice this?" You have the most direct answer in the conversation.

**Suggested comment:**
> "Outcome clarity and contextual framing are the right two. The practice that develops outcome clarity fastest: before opening any AI tool, write one sentence that starts with 'I'll know this is done when...' — it forces you to convert a vague goal into a verifiable condition. Most people skip this step and wonder why the output needs three revision rounds. For contextual framing, the fastest training ground is writing a CLAUDE.md or system prompt for a task you repeat weekly — the discipline of encoding your context in writing, once, reveals every assumption you've been leaving implicit. After doing this for two or three workflows, the skill transfers automatically to live prompting. The underlying pattern both skills share: they move you from describing a request to specifying an outcome. That's the shift that changes how much of the work the agent can carry."
